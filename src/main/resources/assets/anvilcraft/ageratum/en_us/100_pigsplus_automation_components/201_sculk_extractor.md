# Sculk Extractor

<item id="anvilcraft_pigsplus:sculk_extractor"/>

<color=#888888>Can absorb sculk blocks and release large amounts of experience</color>  

> By reverse-engineering the mechanism behind the Sculk Catalyst, the Sculk Extractor was created, capable of reverse-converting sculk blocks into experience orbs

# Function

- Devours connected sculk-related blocks within a distance of 7, generating experience based on the amount
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:echo_cluster"/> provides much more experience (50x)
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:budding_echo_shard"/> can participate in the connection, but will not be consumed and does not grant experience
