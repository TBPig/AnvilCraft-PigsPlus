# Function

- Takes effect while in the player's inventory, with at most one active at a time
- If the player is in AnvilCraft's power grid, continuously consumes 512kW
- Continuously charges FE items in the player's inventory at 40960FE/t

> Converts energy at 1kW ⇒ 80FE/t (same as the base block, adjustable via its config)
