# <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:soulbound_campfire"/>

> A poor imitation of Factorio's biochamber — but it does imitate the biochamber

# Crafting

When a <ref item="minecraft:soul_campfire"/> is lit, it has a chance to turn into a <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:soulbound_campfire"/> based on the number of <ref item="minecraft:weeping_vines"/> nearby

# Operation

- Devours one growth stage from every crop in the surrounding 8 blocks, each granting it 0.2s of burn time

# Uses

- While burning, grants *Regeneration II* and *Health Boost V* to players within 4 blocks
- Can be paired with a <ref item="minecraft:cauldron"/> to perform the *Soul Vine Growth* recipe
- Serves as a campfire to cook food quickly
