<color=#888888>With a single <ref item="anvilcraft:mineral_fountain"/>, you can obtain 8 kinds of metals</color>

> It can be observed that Earth Core Shard Ore is an associated ore of any mineral. By leveraging the affinity of Earth Core Shards for minerals, blending multiple minerals together, and using the Ember Smithing Table's ability to keep their functions independent, you can create a substance that can be smelted into eight kinds of metals.
