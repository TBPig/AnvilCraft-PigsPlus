# <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:soulbound_campfire"/>

<item id="anvilcraft_pigsplus:soulbound_campfire"/>

# Crafting

When a <ref item="minecraft:soul_campfire"/> is lit, it has a chance to turn into a <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:soulbound_campfire"/> based on the number of <ref item="minecraft:twisting_vines"/> nearby (5x5x5)

# Operation

- Devours one growth stage from every crop in the surrounding 8 blocks, each granting it 0.2s of burn time

<warning>
Unabsorbed crops will die, and once they die, they cannot re-ignite automatically.
</warning>

# Uses

- While burning, grants *Regeneration II* and *Health Boost V* to players within 4 blocks
- Can be paired with a <ref item="minecraft:cauldron"/> to perform the *Soul Vine Growth* recipe, producing materials related to necromancy and biological material science
- Serves as a campfire to cook food quickly
