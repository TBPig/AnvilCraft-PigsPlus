# AnvilCraft: Pigs+

Hello! This is Da Zhu. AnvilCraft: Pigs+ adds more blocks and machines to AnvilCraft, making the game more convenient and fun to play.

## Utility Items

- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:weak_resin_block"/>: A more convenient way to capture mobs
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:menger_sponge_staff"/> and <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:menger_sponge_holy_staff"/>: Clear large areas of fluid
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:block_breaker_staff"/>: Break blocks remotely and save inventory space

## Redstone Components

- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:redstone_conduit_block"/>: Transmit redstone signals in 6 directions
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:memory_block_comparator"/>: An <ref item="anvilcraft:block_comparator"/> that needs no extra blocks

## FE Energy Conversion

- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:adjustable_power_converter"/>: Customize the energy conversion ratio between FE and kW
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:portable_wireless_charger"/>: Charge your equipment while inside a power grid
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:grid_adapter"/>: Connect FE blocks to the *AnvilCraft* power grid

## Automation Enhancements

- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:block_breaker"/>: Break blocks
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:brass_sink"/>: An infinite water source
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:auto_chicken"/>: Automatically produce eggs
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:experience_interface"/>: Conveniently store and withdraw experience
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:wireless_transmitter"/>: Transmit items and fluids wirelessly
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:electric_enchanting_table"/>: The ideal solution for enchanting in bulk
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:void_catalyst"/>: Catalyze Void Decay
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:auto_jewel_crafting_table"/>, <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:auto_royal_smithing_table"/>, and <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:auto_royal_grindstone"/>: Automate some AnvilCraft recipes

## Advanced Processing

- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:chain_smithing_table"/>: Complete multiple levels of smithing at once
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:precision_magnetic_pivot"/>, <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:void_acid_bucket"/>, and <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:universal_redstone_component"/>: Assemble electromagnetic products with fewer materials

## Expanded Material Acquisition

- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:chaotic_raw_ore"/>: Obtain 8 metals using a single <ref item="anvilcraft:mineral_fountain"/>
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:budding_echo_shard"/>: Produce <ref item="minecraft:echo_shard"/>
- <ref item="anvilcraft_pigsplus:ender_seed"/>: Produce <ref item="minecraft:ender_pearl"/>
